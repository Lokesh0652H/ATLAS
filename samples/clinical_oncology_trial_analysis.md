# Phase III Comparative Trial Analysis: CAR-T Cell Therapy in Relapsed B-Cell Lymphoma
**Trial Protocol IDs**: NCT-05829104 (AURORA Cohort) & NCT-05994218 (BOREALIS Cohort)  
**Lead Investigators**: Dr. Aris Thorne, Dr. Naomi Chen  
**Publication Source**: Journal of Clinical Cellular Immunology  
**Release Date**: January 2026  

## Executive Summary
Chimeric Antigen Receptor T-cell (CAR-T) immunotherapies targeting CD19 have revolutionized treatment paradigms for adult patients with relapsed or refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL). This analytical dossier evaluates multi-center results from two contemporaneous Phase III registration studies evaluating anti-CD19 autologous construct ACT-1902.

## Study 1: The AURORA Multicenter Trial (NCT-05829104)
**Patient Enrollment**: $N = 340$ adult participants across 28 European academic centers.  
**Preconditioning Regimen**: Fludarabine ($30\text{ mg/m}^2/\text{day}$) and Cyclophosphamide ($300\text{ mg/m}^2/\text{day}$) administered on days -5, -4, and -3 prior to single-dose infusion of $2 \times 10^6$ CAR-positive viable T-cells/kg.

### Primary Efficacy Endpoints (AURORA)
1. **Overall Response Rate (ORR)**: The investigator-assessed ORR achieved **82.4%** (95% CI: 77.8%–86.3%) at month 3.
2. **Complete Remission (CR)**: Complete metabolic response assessed via Lugano PET-CT criteria reached **67.1%** at month 6.
3. **Median Progression-Free Survival (PFS)**: Median PFS was recorded at **24.2 months**, with 58% of patients maintaining durable response at 24 months.
4. **Overall Survival (OS)**: 24-month OS rate was **71.5%**.

### Safety Profile (AURORA)
- **Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS)**: Any-grade CRS occurred in 58% of patients; Grade 3 or 4 CRS occurred in only **3.2%** of patients. Median time to CRS onset was 5 days, resolving within a median duration of 4 days following standard tocilizumab intervention.
- **Immune Effector Cell-Associated Neurotoxicity Syndrome (ICANS)**: Any-grade ICANS was documented in 21% of patients, with Severe Grade $\ge 3$ neurotoxicity observed in **4.1%** of the cohort. No treatment-related mortality was attributed to irreversible cerebral edema.

## Study 2: The BOREALIS Confirmatory Trial (NCT-05994218)
**Patient Enrollment**: $N = 315$ adult participants across 22 North American oncology centers.  
**Preconditioning Regimen**: Identical lymphodepletion dosage of Fludarabine and Cyclophosphamide with autologous ACT-1902 infusion.

### Primary Efficacy Endpoints (BOREALIS)
1. **Overall Response Rate (ORR)**: In striking contrast to earlier European findings, the BOREALIS cohort demonstrated an ORR of only **54.2%** (95% CI: 48.5%–59.8%) at month 3.
2. **Complete Remission (CR)**: Complete remission rate at month 6 was restricted to **38.9%**.
3. **Median Progression-Free Survival (PFS)**: Median PFS was observed at **11.4 months**, with significant disease progression occurring in primary refractory subsets.
4. **Overall Survival (OS)**: 24-month OS rate was recorded at **48.2%**.

### Safety Profile (BOREALIS)
- **Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS)**: Severe Grade 3 or 4 CRS was observed in **14.8%** of patients (over four times the frequency reported in the AURORA cohort), requiring intensive ICU vasopressor support.
- **Neurotoxicity (ICANS)**: Severe Grade $\ge 3$ ICANS manifested in **19.3%** of participants, with 5 patient deaths resulting from refractory status epilepticus and diffuse neuro-inflammation.

---

## Analytical Discussion & Conflicting Evidence
The significant divergence in clinical outcomes between AURORA and BOREALIS presents an acute scientific controversy:
- **Efficacy Discrepancy**: Complete remission rates vary from 67.1% (AURORA) to 38.9% (BOREALIS), and median PFS varies from 24.2 months to 11.4 months.
- **Toxicity Variance**: Severe neurotoxicity (ICANS $\ge 3$) ranges from 4.1% in European centers to 19.3% in North American centers.
- **Investigator Hypotheses**: Potential causative factors include prior exposure to polatuzumab vedotin in the BOREALIS cohort (42% vs 4% in AURORA), variations in cellular manufacturing turnaround time (median 17 days vs 28 days), and baseline lactate dehydrogenase (LDH) elevations indicating higher tumor burden at trial enrollment.
